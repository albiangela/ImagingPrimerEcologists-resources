# Camera Calibration

## Implementation

Overview of camera calibration software supporting single- and multi-camera setups.

| Software | Calibration Method(s) | Reference |
|----------|-----------------------|-----------|
| [multical](https://github.com/oliver-batchelor/multical) | Chessboard calibration | https://github.com/oliver-batchelor/multical |
| [mrcal](https://mrcal.secretsauce.net/) | Chessboard-based with custom intrinsics | https://mrcal.secretsauce.net |
| [Anipose](https://anipose.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorial.html) | Chessboard or Wand calibration | Karashchuk et al. 2021 |
| [Kalibr](https://github.com/ethz-asl/kalibr) | AprilTag-based spatiotemporal calibration | Rehder et al. 2016 |
| [Deep ChArUco](https://github.com/JunkyByte/deepcharuco?tab=readme-ov-file) | Chessboard application for underwater | Du et al. 2019 |

## Notes for contributors

Keep additions concise, clearly labeled, and beginner-friendly.
