# Aerial Imaging with Drones

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs / drones) enable access to previously
unobservable habitats with broad spatial coverage and multi-sensor integration
(RGB, thermal, multi-spectral). Because drones move continuously, camera motion
must be separated from true animal movement; retaining onboard GPS and sensor
logs (commonly `.srt` or `.log` files), flight logs, accurate timestamps, and
photogrammetric products (orthomosaics, digital elevation models) is strongly
recommended for georeferencing detections.

For 3D reconstruction and georeferencing tools, see also
[Photogrammetry](photogrammetry.md).
