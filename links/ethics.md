# Ethics of Imaging

Resources corresponding to §2 of the final manuscript. Plan acquisition with animal welfare, sampling bias, responsible data handling, and resource use in mind.

- **Welfare and refinement:** consider the 3Rs (Replacement, Reduction, Refinement), minimize disturbance, and assess effects of lighting on the study species. [Azevedo et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.3390/ani15223329).
- **Sampling and reporting:** document the STRANGE factors (Social background, Trappability, Rearing history, Acclimation, Natural changes in responsiveness, Genetic make-up, Experience). [Webster & Rutz (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1038/d41586-020-01751-5).
- **Data stewardship:** follow FAIR principles and document acquisition, processing, and provenance. [Wilkinson et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18).
- **Privacy and permissions:** plan how to handle incidental footage of people and consult institutional guidance and applicable local requirements before deployment. [Hoek Spaans et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.251530).
- **Resource use:** assess computing and equipment requirements during study design; document reusable, efficient workflows. Robroek (2024), *Resourceful learning* (doctoral dissertation), [Utz (2025)](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.21720), and [Pearce (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ohx.2020.e00139).
