# Case Studies

Worked examples illustrating how a good understanding of image creation
improves the way data is collected. The subsections below follow Section 6 of
*An Imaging Primer for Ecologists*.

## Standardised Measurements

### Measuring Animals from Images
- Mungee & Athreya (2020) — standardised image correction (rotation, perspective rectification, lens distortion) for morphological measurement of light-attracted moths. See companion notebooks [`02_lens_correction.ipynb`](../notebooks/02_lens_correction.ipynb) and [`03_perspective_rectification_and_scale.ipynb`](../notebooks/03_perspective_rectification_and_scale.ipynb).

### Perspective Correction for Behavioural Observations from Videos
- Gómez-Nava et al. (2023) — perspective rectification with a floating reference object and background subtraction to quantify wave propagation in predator-avoidance schools of sulphur-stream fish. [doi:10.1038/s41567-022-01875-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01875-7)

## Animal Tracking and Behavioural Quantification

### Individual Identification in the Laboratory
- Romero-Ferrero et al. (2023) — overhead recordings and `idtracker.ai` to track individually identified fish and study knowledge transfer between informed and uninformed individuals. See [Animal Tracking](tracking.md).

### Underwater Tracking using Handheld Cameras
- Sampaio et al. (2024) — handheld stereo-vision camera and structure-from-motion to triangulate 3D trajectories of octopus and reef fish during mixed-species foraging. See [Photogrammetry](photogrammetry.md).

## Remote Sensing

### Aerial Imaging with Drones
- McIvor et al. (2022) — drone surveys vs. Baited Remote Underwater Video Systems for elasmobranch abundance in the Red Sea.
- Bennett-Smith et al. (2026) — drone-based aerial views of an aberrantly pigmented cownose ray (*Rhinoptera bonasus*) integrated within a school. [doi:10.1002/ece3.72890](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.72890)
- Sellés-Ríos et al. (2022) — thermal-infrared drones for nocturnal sea-turtle nesting detection.
- See [Aerial Imaging with Drones](georeferencing-drones.md).

### Long-Term Monitoring and Fixed Remote Camera Systems
- Surmacki & Podkowa (2022) — automated trail-camera monitoring of Great Tit (*Parus major*) nest boxes for precise breeding timing.

### Large-Scale Satellite Imagery
- Duporge et al. (2021) — deep learning on very-high-resolution (WorldView-3, 0.31 m) satellite imagery to detect and count African elephants (*Loxodonta africana*). See [Large-Scale Satellite Imagery](satellite-imagery.md).
