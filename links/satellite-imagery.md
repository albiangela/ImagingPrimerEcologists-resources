# Large-Scale Satellite Imagery

## Imagery Providers

Overview of commercial and open-access satellite imagery platforms.

| Provider | Access | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| [Planet Labs](https://www.planet.com/) | Commercial | Daily global coverage, high-resolution multispectral imagery |
| [Copernicus (ESA)](https://www.copernicus.eu/en) | Open access | Free Sentinel-2 multispectral and Sentinel-1 SAR imagery |
| [Maxar](https://www.maxar.com/) | Commercial | Very high-resolution optical imagery (up to 30 cm) |
| [ESA EO Gateway](https://earth.esa.int/eogateway) | Open access | Portal to ESA Earth observation missions, data, and tools |
